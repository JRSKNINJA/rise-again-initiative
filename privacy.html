Privacy and safeguarding

Privacy notice

This is a publication draft and must be reviewed and approved before personal data is collected.

Information and purpose

Website forms may collect name, email, enquiry, volunteer interest and donation intention details voluntarily submitted by visitors. Information is used to respond to enquiries and coordinate activities.

Security and retention

Access must be limited to authorised staff. Define retention periods, deletion procedures, backups and incident response. Never place service-role keys or payment secrets in browser code.

Children and vulnerable people

Do not collect sensitive beneficiary case details through public forms. Implement consent, safeguarding, access controls and a secure casework process before collecting such data.
